EU legislation tracker: where each file stands

Ordinary legislative procedure (co-decision), proposals from 2025 and 2026 placed at their latest recorded stage.

Each number is how many files that institution currently holds. Parliament-side placements come directly from the Parliament's records. The Council box is inferred: it counts files where Parliament has adopted its position and Council approval is not yet recorded. The Council's earlier work, such as agreeing its negotiating position through Coreper, is not in this data. National parliaments and Coreper are shown for context only: this data source has no per-file information on them.

The full procedure at a glance (Art. 294 TFEU)

1 Commission proposalOnly the Commission can propose; sent to Parliament and Council.
→
2 CommitteeRapporteur drafts a report, MEPs table amendments, committee votes.
→
3 NegotiationsParliament and Council negotiate in trilogues to reach a provisional agreement.
→
4 PlenaryParliament votes its first-reading position.
→
5 CouncilCouncil approves Parliament's position: the act is adopted.
→
6 SignaturePresidents of both institutions sign.
→
7 Official JournalPublished; enters into force on the date it sets.
If Council does not approve Parliament's position at first reading: Council adopts its own position, then Parliament's second reading (3 months, extendable by 1) and Council's second reading (3 + 1 months) follow. If they still disagree, a Conciliation Committee has 6 (+2) weeks to agree a joint text, and both institutions then have 6 (+2) weeks for a third reading. Most files never get past the first reading. More on Wikipedia
  • How stages are assigned: each file sits at the stage of its most recent recorded event in the European Parliament's Open Data Portal. The Parliament publishes its own steps; Council steps are not in that data, so stage 5 means “Parliament's position adopted, awaiting Council” and is inferred, and a file the Council has adopted shows as signed only once the signature is recorded.
  • Near real time, not instant. A scheduled job re-reads the Parliament's data every few hours and republishes this page's data file. New events appear once the Parliament records them. The Council's internal steps are not published in this source.
  • Second-reading and conciliation files are flagged on the card where the data marks them.
  • Source: European Parliament Open Data Portal (data.europarl.europa.eu), procedures API v2. Date format is year-month-day.
